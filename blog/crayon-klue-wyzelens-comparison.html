Crayon vs Klue vs WyzeLens

March 11, 2026 | By Labwyze Team

Choosing between Crayon, Klue, and WyzeLens feels like picking between three strong candidates for the same job. They all promise competitive intelligence, but their approaches, pricing, and ideal users couldn't be more different.

This head-to-head comparison cuts through the marketing fluff to help you decide which platform fits your team's size, budget, and intelligence needs.

Quick Overview

Crayon

Best for: Large B2B SaaS teams

Enterprise CI platform focused on tracking website changes and delivering weekly digests. Requires 2-4 weeks onboarding. Starting price: ~$15k/year.

Klue

Best for: Sales enablement teams

Sales-focused CI tool with CRM integrations and pre-built battlecards. Setup time: 1-3 weeks. Starting price: ~$12k/year.

WyzeLens

Best for: Startups, SMBs, agile teams

AI-powered CI automation with real-time tracking and auto-discovery. Setup time: <3 minutes. Starting price: Free / $8/month.

Pricing Breakdown

Crayon Pricing

Custom pricing only (typically $15,000-$30,000+/year). Enterprise focus with annual contracts. Implementation fees often apply. Best for teams with 50+ employees.

Klue Pricing

Custom pricing (starts around $12,000/year). Tiered by number of users with annual contracts preferred. Add-ons for premium integrations. Target: sales teams of 20+.

WyzeLens Pricing

Transparent, self-serve pricing:

  • Free: $0 (1 profile, 5 competitors, 7 days history)
  • Starter: $8/month (3 profiles, daily refresh)
  • Pro: $20/month (5 profiles, hourly refresh)
  • Business: $49/month (10 profiles, unlimited competitors)

No annual lock-in. Cancel anytime. View full pricing →

Which Tool Should You Choose?

Choose Crayon if: You're an enterprise (500+ employees) with $20k+/year budget, need battlecards, and have resources for 2-4 week onboarding.

Choose Klue if: Sales enablement is your primary goal, you need CRM integrations (Salesforce/HubSpot), budget is $12k+/year, and win/loss tracking is critical.

Choose WyzeLens if: You're a startup, SMB, or agile team with limited budget ($0-$500/month), want AI-powered automation, and need to start tracking competitors today.

Final Verdict

There's no universal "best" CI tool—it depends on your team size, budget, and priorities. Crayon and Klue excel in enterprise environments with dedicated sales teams and large budgets. WyzeLens is the modern alternative for teams that want fast, AI-powered intelligence without the enterprise overhead.
